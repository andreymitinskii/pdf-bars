OCR language models are from the official Tesseract repositories:

- rus.traineddata: high-accuracy model from tessdata_best
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best
- eng.traineddata: compact model from tessdata
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata
